Exchange mini app

Six v1 screens, drawn with Telegram's own theme colours. Sample data: EUR/RUB in one group, sample names and amounts.

Has EUR to give (amber, never red) Wants EUR (teal, never green) Range bars: the band a counterparty accepts, with a dark tick for your amount. Blue buttons and links are Telegram's own theme colours, so the app matches whichever client theme you use.
